## Microsoft Entra (Azure) discovery

When you face (are tasked with) Azure/Entra discovery or cataloging, and want to work in an efficient manner, be sure to use the following:

```powershell
Get-AzResource
```

along with:

```powershell
Get-AzSubscription
```
